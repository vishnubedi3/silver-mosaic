# Project Archive Index (Historical & Non-Referential Artifacts)

This directory serves as the isolated historical archive for the `silver-mosaic` repository and its research deliverables. 

## Archive Policy & Non-Referential Designation

All documents residing in `/research/archive/` are classified as:

- **Status:** `ARCHIVED`
- **Designation:** `NON-REFERENTIAL`

### Operational Rules for Archived Artifacts:
1. **Traceability Only:** Retained solely for auditability, provenance tracking, and version history.
2. **Non-Authoritative:** Must **not** be cited as an authoritative source in any ongoing or future research.
3. **Non-Influential:** Must **not** influence future research or revisions.
4. **Isolated from Active Workspace:** Active working documents must not link to or depend upon archived files.
5. **Single Source of Truth:** The **Current Working Draft** at `research/notes/final_report_zero-budget-fields.md` is the project's single authoritative source of truth.
